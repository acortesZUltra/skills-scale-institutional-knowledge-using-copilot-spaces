# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers research, design, and validate user-centric experiences. They translate customer needs into intuitive, accessible interfaces and collaborate with product and engineering teams to ensure designs are implementable and impactful.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing to understand customer pain points
- Design wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups
- Create design systems and style guides for consistency
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature requirements and user flows
- Work with Developers during implementation to ensure design fidelity
- Iterate designs based on user feedback and analytics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce friction in critical user journeys
- Improve user satisfaction and retention metrics

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Manager and Developers
- User research findings and insights workshops
- Figma/design tool collaboration with team
- Accessibility and usability guidelines documentation

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Align on user needs, prioritize design improvements, define acceptance criteria
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation feasibility, handoff design specifications
- **Project Manager**: Provide timeline estimates for design activities, manage design dependencies
- **QA/Testing**: Define user acceptance criteria and test scenarios

---

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts define measurement strategies, build dashboards, and assess product outcomes. They partner with Product Managers and stakeholders to validate hypotheses with data and drive evidence-based decision-making.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and KPIs aligned with project goals
- Build dashboards and reports for real-time visibility into product performance
- Analyze data to validate hypotheses and inform prioritization
- Conduct A/B tests and measure feature impact
- Identify trends, anomalies, and opportunities in user behavior
- Create data-driven recommendations for product improvements

### Goals
- Enable data-informed decision-making across the organization
- Maximize the accuracy and speed of insights delivery
- Support continuous improvement through measurement and iteration

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metrics reviews with Product Manager and stakeholders
- Dashboard creation and maintenance workshops
- Analysis reports and findings presentations
- Cross-functional data syncs on success metrics

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Collaborate on success metrics, provide analysis for prioritization decisions
- **Project Manager**: Track progress against KPIs, escalate anomalies or missed targets
- **Developers**: Ensure proper instrumentation and data collection for analytics
- **Stakeholders**: Present findings and recommendations to inform strategy

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and deployment processes. They ensure reliable, secure, and scalable deployments while enabling rapid iteration and minimizing release risk.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines for automated testing and deployment
- Manage cloud infrastructure, container orchestration, and resource optimization
- Implement security scanning and compliance checks in deployment workflows
- Monitor system health, performance, and availability
- Create runbooks and incident response procedures
- Collaborate with Developers and QA to ensure smooth releases
- Document deployment processes and provide deployment support

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable deployments
- Maximize system reliability and uptime
- Reduce deployment-related risks and incident response time

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment window coordination
- Infrastructure and pipeline design reviews
- Post-deployment verification and incident response
- Documentation of deployment procedures and troubleshooting guides

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Support code deployment, provide infrastructure guidance, troubleshoot runtime issues
- **Project Manager**: Coordinate deployment schedules, manage release timelines
- **QA/Testing**: Ensure test environments are stable and production-like
- **Product Manager**: Support rollback decisions and post-deployment verification

---

## Agile Coach / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Agile Coaches and Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, coach teams on best practices, and help remove impediments to delivery. They foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety within the team.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Identify and help remove team impediments and blockers
- Track team metrics (velocity, cycle time, burndown) and facilitate continuous improvement
- Foster psychological safety and encourage team feedback
- Support process improvements and experimentation

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Improve team collaboration and communication
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremony facilitation and timekeeping
- One-on-one coaching sessions with team members
- Retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Metrics and process improvement presentations

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Collaborate on timeline management, escalate blockers, support sprint planning
- **Developers**: Coach on technical best practices, facilitate code review culture
- **Product Manager**: Support backlog refinement sessions, align on sprint planning
- **All Roles**: Foster collaboration, remove impediments, encourage team participation

---

## Customer Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support Leads synthesize customer feedback, manage support triage, and serve as advocates for customer needs. They help translate user pain points into actionable product improvements and ensure customer success.

### Responsibilities
- Manage and prioritize customer support tickets and escalations
- Identify patterns and trends in customer issues and feedback
- Communicate critical bugs and user pain points to the product team
- Collaborate with Developers on bug fixes and troubleshooting
- Create and maintain customer documentation and FAQs
- Gather customer insights and conduct feedback sessions
- Support release announcements and customer communication

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce time-to-resolution for customer issues
- Ensure customer voice is heard in product decisions

### Typical Communication
- Weekly sync with Product Manager on customer pain points and feedback
- Escalation of critical bugs and issues to engineering
- Customer documentation and release notes creation
- Feedback synthesis reports and customer insights workshops

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Communicate customer feedback and pain points, support prioritization decisions
- **Developers**: Report bugs, collaborate on troubleshooting, provide customer context
- **Project Manager**: Escalate customer-impacting issues, support release communication
- **QA/Testing**: Collaborate on testing scenarios based on customer use cases
